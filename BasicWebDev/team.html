About the Team

We are a diverse group of houseplants specializing in air purification, aesthetic improvement, and occasionally dropping leaves when we're stressed.

Monty the Monstera

Monty is the visionary of the group. With large, bold leaves and a thirst for sunlight, he leads the team in aesthetic growth and architectural presence.

Sylvia the Snake Plant

Sylvia is the team's resilience expert. She can survive almost anything and works hard overnight to clean the air for the rest of the office.

Penny the Pothos

Penny is our communications officer. She loves to reach out and connect with other areas of the room through her long, trailing vines.

Felix the Fiddle Leaf

Felix is the high-maintenance strategist. He requires precise conditions to succeed, but when he does, he provides a stunning focal point for the whole team.

Care Guide

The team follows a strict regime of indirect sunlight, filtered water, and occasional misting.
