How to Embed a Video on a Website

Follow these steps to embed a service-hosted video into your web page. Each step includes a screenshot showing the action.

Step 1: Open the video page

Go to the video you want to embed and make sure it allows embedding.

Step 2: Click the Share button

Under the video, click Share to open the sharing options.

Step 3: Choose Embed and copy the code

From the share menu, click Embed, then copy the HTML code shown in the embed window.

Step 4: Paste the code into your web page

Paste the code into the HTML where you want the video to appear.
