The centre of our system

The Sun

The Sun is a star made mostly of hydrogen and helium. Its gravity holds the Solar System together, while its energy warms our planet and powers weather and photosynthesis.

Deep inside the Sun, nuclear fusion turns hydrogen into helium and releases enormous amounts of energy. That energy travels outward and eventually reaches Earth as sunlight.

Layers of the Sun

  • Core: where nuclear fusion takes place.
  • Radiative zone: energy moves outward mainly as radiation.
  • Convective zone: hot plasma rises and cooler plasma sinks.
  • Photosphere: the visible surface of the Sun.
  • Corona: the Sun's very hot outer atmosphere.

Never look directly at the Sun. Safe solar viewing requires specially designed, certified solar filters.
