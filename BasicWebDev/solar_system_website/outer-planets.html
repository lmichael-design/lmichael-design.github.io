The giant worlds

The Outer Planets

Beyond the asteroid belt are four giant planets. Jupiter and Saturn are gas giants, while Uranus and Neptune are ice giants.

Jupiter

Jupiter is a gas giant with many moons. Its Great Red Spot is a huge storm that has lasted for centuries.

Saturn

Saturn's rings are made mostly of pieces of ice and rock. The planet is less dense than water.

Uranus

Uranus is an ice giant with a blue-green colour caused by methane in its atmosphere. It has an extreme axial tilt.

Neptune

Neptune is the most distant recognised planet from the Sun. It has very fast winds and a deep blue appearance.

How are the outer planets different?

  • Gas giants
    • Jupiter
    • Saturn
  • Ice giants
    • Uranus
    • Neptune
  • Shared features
    • All have rings, although some are faint.
    • All have multiple known moons.
