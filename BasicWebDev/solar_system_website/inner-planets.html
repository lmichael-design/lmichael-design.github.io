The Inner Planets

The four inner planets are relatively small and have solid surfaces. They are Mercury, Venus, Earth and Mars.

Mercury

Mercury is closest to the Sun. It has a heavily cratered surface and almost no atmosphere to retain heat.

Venus

Venus has a thick atmosphere rich in carbon dioxide. Its powerful greenhouse effect makes it hotter than Mercury.

Earth

Earth has liquid water on its surface and a protective atmosphere. It is the only planet known to have life.

Mars

Mars is cold and dusty, with a thin atmosphere. Scientists study it for clues about whether microbial life may once have existed there.
