Solar System Facts & Quiz

Here are a few surprising facts about our cosmic neighbourhood. Then try the short quiz to check your knowledge.

A giant storm

Jupiter's Great Red Spot is a massive storm system that has been observed for hundreds of years.

Long days

Venus rotates so slowly that one rotation takes longer than one orbit around the Sun.

Many moons

Several planets have large families of moons, each with different landscapes and histories.
