Discovering beyond Earth

Space Exploration

Robotic spacecraft, telescopes and human missions help us understand the Solar System. They collect images and measurements that would be impossible to obtain from Earth alone.

Exploration has revealed volcanoes on other worlds, oceans beneath icy moons and details about the atmospheres of distant planets.

Why explore space?

  • To understand how planets and stars form.
  • To search for environments that could support life.
  • To develop new technology and scientific skills.
  • To learn more about Earth's place in the universe.
